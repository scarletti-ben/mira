Mira Makadia is a freelance Editor-Researcher working across publishing, heritage and visual culture. 

She is currently an Image Researcher at Taschen, working primarily on photography, art and design titles. From 2022 to 2024, she served as Co-Editor-in-Chief of GIDA Journal, an Africa-focused arts and culture magazine. She has participated in panels including New African Print: Editors Q&A (2023) and Museum of Youth Culture Symposium on community-rooted archival practice (2021). 

Presently, she is engaged in independent research examining sexual representation, collective fantasy and South Asian identity in ‘post-colonial’ Britain.
